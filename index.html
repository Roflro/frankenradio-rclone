FrankenRadio-rclone

This is a personal, non-commercial OAuth configuration used with rclone to access and synchronize the owner's Google Drive files across their own computers.

It is not a public application, radio streaming service, or a service offered to other users.

Google Drive access

The owner authorizes rclone to read, create, modify, and delete files in their Google Drive. Access is used solely at the owner's direction to manage personal project files. No third-party users are invited to connect their accounts.

For information about handling of data, see the Privacy Policy.
