Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

FrankenRadio-rclone is a personal OAuth configuration for the open-source tool rclone, operated by the owner of this GitHub repository for access to their own Google Drive account. It is not offered as a service to the public.

Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization, rclone may access Google Drive files and metadata and may read, create, change, or delete files as directed by the owner.

Use and storage

Access is used to manage and synchronize the owner's files between their own computers and Google Drive. OAuth credentials and local cached data are stored on the owner's devices as part of the rclone configuration and operation. This website does not collect Google account data, and no Google Drive content or OAuth credentials are published in this repository.

Sharing

The operator does not sell Google user data or provide it to advertisers. Data is handled by Google Drive and rclone as necessary for the owner's requested file operations. The owner may separately choose to share individual files using Google Drive.

Security and control

The owner controls the connected account and can revoke access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Deleting local rclone credentials or revoking authorization stops further authorized access until reconnected.

Contact

Questions about this personal integration can be raised through the GitHub repository's Issues page.
